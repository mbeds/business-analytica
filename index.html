Data & Methodology

We provide actionable intelligence from raw corporate and registry records.

Public listings only surface deals after brokers are already instructed. Business Analytica cross-examines corporate filings and underlying asset titles to provide actionable intelligence so buyers, brokers, and advisors can act early.

Where the Data Comes From

Compiled from official public registries: Companies House (corporate status, SIC sector classifications, active directors, mortgage and charge registers, and filing or insolvency notices) cross-referenced with HM Land Registry title deeds, Scottish land registers, and sector asset datasets.

How It Is Processed

Entities, officers, registered charges, and physical holdings are linked and scored against asset valuations, estimated cash-flow or rent yield, 75% LTV borrowing headroom, historic acquisition intervals, and your custom sector or geographic filters to provide actionable intelligence.

How Much Time It Saves

Manually auditing hundreds of company charge histories, director cross-appointments, and title registers across a territory or sector takes weeks. We deliver actionable intelligence directly to your desk within 24 hours so you move straight to execution.

Deliverables & Who Uses It

Each report includes up to 25 executive PDF dossiers and up to 100 structured CSV records per side—used by investors, corporate acquisition teams, deal sourcers, sector brokers, restructuring advisors, and private equity.
